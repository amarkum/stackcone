## Why is iPhone Duo so expensive in India?

India’s Duo price is not “Apple being mean for fun.” It stacks **import economics + GST + pricing buffer**.

### 1. Finished foldables are still import-heavy

Unlike many slab iPhones now assembled in India, early Duo units are widely treated as **imported finished devices**. That pulls in:

- **Basic Customs Duty (BCD)** on smartphones (commonly cited ~15% on CIF for fully built units in recent analyses)
- **Social Welfare Surcharge** on the duty
- Then **IGST / GST at 18%** on (value + duty)

One India tax walkthrough starting from a ~₹1.90 lakh US-equivalent CIF roughly lands near **₹2.6 lakh** after duty + GST math — *before* Apple’s final round MRP of **₹2,99,900**.

### 2. 18% GST is inside the MRP

The ₹2,99,900 you see already includes GST. US $1,999 does **not** include sales tax. Fair comparisons always tax-adjust.

### 3. FX buffer and premium positioning

Apple often prices India with a cushion for INR volatility and keeps round rupee ladders (₹25k / ₹50k storage steps on Duo). Foldables also sit in a **halo** tier — Duo undercuts nothing; it sits above Galaxy Fold / Pixel Fold street prices in India on purpose.

### 4. Channel and compliance costs

BIS, packaging, warranty logistics, and authorised retail margins add friction on a low-volume first-gen foldable.

### 5. Same story as slab Pros — amplified

18 Pro / Pro Max are also dearer in India than the US after tax math. Duo’s absolute gap looks larger simply because the base USD sticker is already **$1,999**.

**Bottom line:** Duties + 18% GST + Apple’s India list strategy explain most of the **~₹1 lakh** headline gap vs a US pre-tax conversion — not a secret “India tax” unique to foldables alone.

## Should Indians buy Duo abroad?

Only if **landed cost** wins:

Add card forex (~1–3.5%), possible Indian customs if assessed, warranty friction (service may prefer local purchase), dual-eSIM / region quirks, and travel cost.

Rough math: US tax-paid ~₹2.04L + 2% forex ≈ **₹2.08L** before customs risk — on paper ~₹90k under India. That gap collapses if customs bites or you book a trip only for the phone.

**Safer play for most buyers:** buy in India from Apple / authorised partners; use bank offers and exchange deals. Use overseas only as a **side quest on an existing trip** to US / HK / Dubai.

## How to spend less in India

1. Apple / authorised only for first-gen foldable warranty peace.
2. Stack card cashback + exchange bonuses (often tens of thousands effective on ~₹3L carts).
3. Skip 2TB unless you need it — **₹1.5 lakh** jump from 256GB to 2TB in India.
4. Honestly compare **18 Pro Max ₹1,79,900** if you want screen size without the fold tax.
5. Wait a quarter if supply/pricing softens — first Apple foldable demand is supply-constrained early on.
