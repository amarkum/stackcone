# How to Get the Best USD Exchange Rate from Upwork (India)

**June 2026 · Published by Amar Kumar**

Your Upwork hourly rate is only half the story. **How you withdraw USD** can cost you ₹1,000–₹3,000 extra per **$1,000** — before income tax.

After testing Payoneer, Wise, direct bank transfer, and **[Infinity](https://www.infinityapp.in/)**, we settled on **Infinity** for Upwork payouts: **0.5% all-inclusive**, **0% FX markup**, live/Google rates, ~1-day INR settlement, and free FIRA.

## Best method: US virtual account + ACH

Upwork's **Direct to U.S. Bank (ACH)** withdrawal is **free** from Upwork's side. Pair it with an Indian fintech that gives you a **US routing + account number**:

1. Client pays → Upwork deducts commission
2. Upwork sends **USD via free ACH** to your virtual US account (Infinity, Wise, Mulya, etc.)
3. Provider converts USD → INR at their rate
4. INR lands in your Indian bank + FIRA issued

This beats **Direct to Local Bank** where Upwork converts at a marked-up rate you cannot control.

## Setup: Infinity + Upwork (step by step)

1. Sign up at [infinityapp.in](https://www.infinityapp.in/) → complete KYC (PAN, Aadhaar, bank)
2. Open your **USD receiving account** — note routing number + account number
3. Upwork → **Settings** → **Get Paid** → **Add method**
4. Choose **Direct to U.S. Bank (ACH)** — not "Direct to Local Bank"
5. Enter Infinity's US account details → save → wait for verification (~1–3 days)
6. Withdraw USD from Upwork → Infinity converts at live rate → INR in your bank
7. Download **FIRA** from Infinity dashboard for GST/records

## Wise setup (alternative)

1. Open Wise → get US account details
2. Add as **Direct to U.S. Bank** on Upwork (free ACH)
3. USD lands in Wise → convert to INR manually when rate is good
4. Fee shown upfront (~1–2% on USD→INR)

Payoneer: add as payment method if already integrated — check effective rate on each withdrawal; often worse than ACH + Infinity/Wise.

## What to avoid

- **PayPal** from Upwork — high fees + poor FX (often 4%+ total drag)
- **SWIFT wire to Indian bank** — $15–40 per transfer + correspondent cuts
- **Converting immediately** on bad FX days — some platforms let you hold USD (Mulya, Wise, Infinity)
