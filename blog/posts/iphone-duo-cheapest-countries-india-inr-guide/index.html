Why is iPhone Duo so expensive in India?

India’s Duo price is not random. It stacks import economics + GST + pricing buffer.

1. Finished foldables are still import-heavy

Unlike many slab iPhones now assembled in India, early Duo units are widely treated as imported finished devices. That pulls in basic customs duty on smartphones, social welfare surcharge on the duty, then IGST / GST at 18% on (value + duty).

One India tax walkthrough starting from a ~₹1.90 lakh US-equivalent CIF roughly lands near ₹2.6 lakh after duty + GST math — before Apple’s final round MRP of ₹2,99,900.

2. 18% GST is inside the MRP

The ₹2,99,900 you see already includes GST. US $1,999 does not include sales tax. Fair comparisons always tax-adjust.

3. FX buffer and premium positioning

Apple often prices India with a cushion for INR volatility and keeps round rupee ladders (₹25k / ₹50k storage steps on Duo). Foldables also sit in a halo tier above most Android foldable street prices in India.

4. Channel and compliance costs

BIS, packaging, warranty logistics, and authorised retail margins add friction on a low-volume first-gen foldable.

5. Same Pro story — amplified

18 Pro / Pro Max are also dearer in India than the US after tax math. Duo’s absolute gap looks larger simply because the base USD sticker is already $1,999.

Should Indians buy Duo abroad?

Only if landed cost wins. Add card forex (~1–3.5%), possible Indian customs if assessed, warranty friction, eSIM/region quirks, and travel cost.

Rough math: US tax-paid ~₹2.04L + 2% forex ≈ ₹2.08L before customs risk — on paper ~₹90k under India. That gap collapses if customs bites or you book a trip only for the phone.

Safer play for most buyers: buy in India from Apple / authorised partners. Use overseas only as a side quest on an existing trip to the US, Hong Kong, or Dubai.

How to spend less in India

  1. Apple / authorised only for first-gen foldable warranty peace.
  2. Stack card cashback + exchange bonuses (often tens of thousands effective on ~₹3L carts).
  3. Skip 2TB unless you need it — ₹1.5 lakh jump from 256GB to 2TB in India.
  4. Honestly compare 18 Pro Max at ₹1,79,900 if you want screen size without the fold tax.
  5. Wait a quarter if supply/pricing softens — early Duo demand is supply-constrained.
