# Stop Upwork GST Deduction (India)

**June 2026 · Published by Amar Kumar**

If you earn **$1,000 on Upwork**, you are **not** paying 18% GST on the full $1,000. Upwork charges **10% freelancer service fee** ($100), then **18% GST on that fee only** ($18) — unless you add a valid **GSTIN** to your tax settings.

This guide explains the math, the confusion about billing clients, how to **stop Upwork from collecting GST**, step-by-step **GST registration**, and using a **residential address in Bangalore** when you own the home.

> **Not tax advice.** Rules change. Confirm with a CA before filing. Sources: [Upwork Help](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/360041114713), GST portal, Bengaluru GST zone notices.

## What Upwork actually taxes {#what-upwork-taxes}

Per [Upwork’s India GST help article](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/360041114713):

- GST applies to **Upwork’s services to you** — membership, Connects, and **freelancer service fees**
- Rate: **18% of the taxed service cost** (not 18% of client payment)
- Upwork collects and remits to the Indian government **unless you provide a valid GSTIN**

## The $1,000 example {#thousand-dollar-example}

| Line | Amount |
|------|--------|
| Client pays you | $1,000 |
| Upwork commission (10%) | −$100 |
| GST on commission (18% of $100) | −$18 |
| **You receive (before forex)** | **$882** |

Without GSTIN, Upwork takes **$118** in fees+tax on the commission slice, not **$180**.

## GST on Upwork fees vs GST on your client invoice {#two-different-gsts}

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Does Upwork GST mean I must charge clients 18% extra? | **No** — not automatically |
| Foreign client (export of services) | Invoice at **0%** with valid **LUT** — do **not** add 18% to client bill |
| Indian domestic client | **Domestic supply** — generally charge **18% GST** on your fee (even if paid via Upwork in USD) |
| Upwork taking $18 GST | Tax on **platform fee**, separate from your client invoice |

## Foreign client vs Indian client {#foreign-vs-indian}

**Foreign client (usual Upwork case):** Place of supply is outside India → export of services → zero-rated with LUT → client pays your USD rate only.

**Indian client:** Place of supply is India → charge 18% GST on your professional fee. Currency (USD vs INR) does not make it an export.

## How to stop Upwork GST deduction {#stop-deduction}

1. Register for GST and get **GSTIN** (15 characters, e.g. `29ABCDE1234F1Z5`)
2. Upwork → **Settings** → **Tax information**
3. Click pencil icon → enter GSTIN → **Save**
4. Upwork validates GSTIN; once accepted, **stops collecting 18% GST** on fees

**Past GST already paid to government via Upwork is not refunded** — may be claimable as credit with CA help.

## What happens after you add GSTIN (RCM) {#rcm-after-gstin}

Upwork is a **foreign service provider**. When they stop collecting GST, **you** may owe **Reverse Charge Mechanism (RCM)** — 18% on Upwork fees + Connects, paid via GST portal, often claimable as **ITC**.

Typical monthly loop: calculate RCM → pay cash challan → GSTR-3B → self-invoice → claim ITC.

## Do you need GST registration? {#do-you-need-gst}

Common reasons Indian Upwork freelancers register:

- Stop Upwork collecting GST on fees (GSTIN required)
- File **LUT** for zero-rated exports to foreign clients
- Claim **ITC** on RCM and business expenses
- Mandatory if turnover crosses threshold (₹20L services in most states; ₹10L special category states)

Many full-time Upwork freelancers register even below turnover because of platform fees + RCM.

## Step-by-step: get a GST number {#gst-registration-steps}

### Before you start

- **PAN** (individual)
- **Aadhaar** linked to PAN
- **Mobile + email** for OTP
- **Bank account** in your name
- **Address proof** for place of business (see Bangalore section)

### Steps on gst.gov.in

1. Go to [gst.gov.in](https://www.gst.gov.in/) → **Services** → **Registration** → **New Registration**
2. Select **Taxpayer** → state **Karnataka** → district → legal name as per PAN
3. Enter PAN → validate → OTP on mobile/email
4. **Temporary Reference Number (TRN)** → continue application within 15 days
5. **Business details:** Legal name, trade name, constitution (Individual/Proprietorship), date of commencement
6. **Promoter/partner:** PAN, Aadhaar, photograph, residential address
7. **Principal place of business:** Pin Bangalore address on map → enter full address
8. **Upload address proof** (owned home: electricity bill — see below)
9. **Bank details:** cancelled cheque or statement
10. **Authorize signatory** (yourself for sole proprietor)
11. **Verification:** DSC or Aadhaar-based e-sign
12. **ARN** issued → officer may verify → **GSTIN** on approval (often 3–7 working days; varies)

## Residential address in Bangalore (owned home) {#bangalore-residential}

**Yes — you can register GST at a residential address** if you work from home.

### If you **own** the home (your name on documents)

Upload **any one** of (per GST portal / Bengaluru zone guidance):

- Latest **property tax receipt**
- **Municipal khata** copy
- **Electricity bill** in your name (ideally recent, within ~2 months)

**NOC is not required** when you own the premises and submit valid ownership proof. NOC/consent letters apply when you use someone else’s property (rented without agreement, parent’s house in their name only, shared consent arrangement).

Bengaluru GST zone has published grievance redressal notices clarifying that officers should not demand documents beyond the prescribed list (property tax, khata, electricity bill, rent agreement where applicable).

### If you **rent**

- Registered **rent/lease agreement** + landlord ownership proof, **or**
- Electricity bill in **tenant’s name** + rent agreement

### Tips for Bangalore freelancers

- Pin exact location on GST map (important for jurisdiction)
- Keep a dedicated workspace photo ready if verification is scheduled
- GST Seva Kendra, Queens Road, Bengaluru for in-person help if application stalls

## Monthly compliance after registration {#compliance}

1. **LUT** — file at start of FY if exporting to foreign clients (Form GST RFD-11)
2. **Invoices** — export at 0% with LUT ref; domestic with 18%
3. **RCM on Upwork fees** — calculate monthly, pay via challan
4. **GSTR-1** — outward supplies (if applicable)
5. **GSTR-3B** — summary return + RCM reporting
6. **Keep Upwork fee invoices** — for RCM and ITC

## FAQ {#faq}

**Does 18% GST on Upwork mean I add 18% to my client rate?**  
No for foreign clients (export + LUT). Yes for Indian domestic clients on your professional fee.

**Can I stop GST without registering?**  
No. GSTIN is required for Upwork to stop collecting.

**Is GST on $1,000 or on Upwork’s $100 fee?**  
On the **$100 service fee** → $18.

**Will Upwork refund GST I already paid?**  
No. Upwork remitted it to the government.

**Residential GST in Bangalore without commercial office?**  
Allowed with valid home address proof; owned home + electricity bill typically needs no NOC.

## Glossary {#glossary}

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **GSTIN** | 15-digit GST identification number |
| **RCM** | Reverse Charge — you pay GST on imported services (Upwork fees) |
| **LUT** | Letter of Undertaking — export invoices at 0% without charging IGST |
| **ITC** | Input Tax Credit — offset GST paid on inputs |
| **Place of supply** | Determines domestic vs export treatment |
