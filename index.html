Frequently asked questions

What custom software development services does stackcone offer?

We build production AI agents and AI automation for business, agentic RAG with LLMOps evals, support and ops copilots, document intelligence, data engineering, Python full-stack web (React, Next.js, FastAPI, Django), Flutter mobile, and cloud DevOps on AWS, GCP, and Azure — with fixed scope and full handover.

Do you work with clients globally?

Yes — remote-first worldwide. We schedule overlapping hours where needed, agree milestones in writing, and hand over documentation your team can run after the engagement.

How do I hire AI developers or a custom software team at stackcone?

Use our contact form, hire us on Upwork, or message us on LinkedIn. We respond within 24 hours with discovery and fixed-scope next steps.

Do you build AI agents for business automation?

Yes — production AI agents and multi-agent workflows with LangGraph, MCP tool servers, human handoff, evaluation gates, and tracing so agents run real business tasks. Browse solution briefs for agent architectures.

Can you build a production RAG or agentic RAG system?

Yes — document ingestion, vector search, reranking, tool-using retrieval, evaluation, monitoring, and deployment. See our portfolio and blog for case studies and technical guides on production RAG.

Do you do Python full-stack development?

Yes — FastAPI, Django, and Flask backends with React or Next.js frontends, PostgreSQL, REST/GraphQL APIs, and cloud deploy on AWS, GCP, and Azure. AI features can ship inside the same product stack.

How do you handle data privacy and AI governance?

Privacy-aware by design: minimize data processed, support regional cloud residency when required, and ship agents with human-in-the-loop controls, audit trails, and access boundaries. We align to your compliance requirements — we do not claim certifications we do not hold. See our privacy policy.

Do you migrate cloud data warehouses and data platforms?

Yes — phased migrations with dbt transform ports, Kafka/CDC ingestion rebuilds, Airflow/Composer orchestration, historical backfill, parallel-run reconciliation, and cutover across warehouses like Snowflake and BigQuery. See our data platform migration brief.

Can you add evaluation and LLMOps to our AI agents?

Yes — eval harnesses, regression gates, tracing, cost and latency dashboards, and safety checks so agents and RAG systems ship with measurable quality. Browse solution briefs for agent and RAG architectures.

What is the difference between a RAG chatbot, a workflow agent, and a copilot?

A RAG chatbot answers from your documents with citations and an explicit “I don’t know” path. A workflow agent takes actions in your tools — CRM, tickets, databases — with approvals and tracing. A copilot sits inside an existing product and assists a person in place. Most teams start with RAG, add tools when the bot needs to do work, and ship a copilot when the UI already exists.
