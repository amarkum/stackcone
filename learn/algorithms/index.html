Course content

  1. 1 Searching Linear search, binary search and how to search an answer space. 13 min
  2. 2 Sorting Bubble, merge and quick sort, plus stability and what Python really uses. 18 min
  3. 3 Recursion Base cases, recursive cases, the call stack and when to avoid recursion. 15 min
  4. 4 Greedy Algorithms Make the locally best choice at each step, and know when that is enough. 14 min
  5. 5 Backtracking Explore choices, undo them, and generate permutations and subsets. 16 min
  6. 6 Dynamic Programming Overlapping subproblems, memoization, tabulation, knapsack and edit distance. 20 min

What you will learn

  • Write binary search without off-by-one bugs
  • Compare O(n^2) and O(n log n) sorts
  • Identify base and recursive cases
  • Explain the greedy choice property
  • Use choose-explore-unchoose
  • Recognize DP problems

How it works

Every lesson explains the idea in plain language, shows small runnable examples with their output, and ends with an exercise and a worked solution.
