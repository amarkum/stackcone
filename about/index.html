About Us

stackcone is a full-stack software and AI engineering studio. We design, build, and deploy production systems for startups and enterprise — one team from discovery to handover.

Who we are

We are a remote-first development studio focused on production AI, data platforms, and custom software. Our work spans AI agents and RAG with eval gates, support and ops copilots, document intelligence, Snowflake to BigQuery migrations, dbt pipelines, full-stack web apps, Flutter mobile, and cloud infrastructure.

stackcone was built on a simple idea: clients should get senior engineering without the overhead of a large agency. Every project gets clear scope, direct communication, working demos, and documentation your team can maintain after handover. We serve teams worldwide — from early-stage startups shipping an MVP to enterprise groups modernizing internal tools.

10+ years experience · 30+ projects delivered · All 5-star rated · 100% job success · Expert-vetted on Upwork
